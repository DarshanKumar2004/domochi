domochi game: privacy

Last updated November 1, 2026. This covers the domochi mochi donut game, on the web and as the Android app.

The short version

The game has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your progress stays on your phone. The only thing that ever leaves it is a note you choose to send to domochi.

What stays on your device

  • Your game progress: the day you're on, money, supplies, stickers, journal pages and settings.
  • The name you give if you join the team in the game (late game).
  • A random code made on your device, so coupon codes stay the same for you. It isn't linked to who you are.

None of this is sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app (or clearing the site's data) deletes it.

What you can choose to send

On the home screen, "Suggest a flavor" and "Let Ethan know" send your message, and a first name if you add one, to domochi by email. It's only sent when you tap Send. Please don't put private information in a note.

To deliver it, the note passes through a small relay on Cloudflare and is emailed with Resend. They handle it only to deliver the email. domochi reads the notes and doesn't share or sell them.

Children

The game doesn't ask for anyone's personal information. A note's text is whatever its writer types, so we ask that kids don't include personal details.

Contact

Questions, or want a note you sent removed? Message domochi on Instagram at @domochi.treats or through domochitreats.com.
