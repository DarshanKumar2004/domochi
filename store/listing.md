**Full description** (4000 characters max)

Welcome to domochi, Lafayette's first mochi donut shop! Step into the trailer and make chewy, pull-apart mochi donuts for a line of hungry customers.

🍩 Make every donut by hand
- Mix the mochi dough and stir it smooth
- Fry each ring golden: flip it, hold it under, pull it at just the right time
- Pour glazes and drizzles along the loops
- Shake on toppings, then box every order just right

☀️ Run the trailer
- A new day every five minutes, with morning pickups for coffee shops
- Buy supplies at the store, and don't run out (a friend might swing by if you do)
- Sunny, cloudy, hot and rainy days that change how the day goes
- Regulars who learn your name and order "the usual"

🌸 Grow your little business
- Unlock real domochi flavors: beignet, strawberry, cookies & cream, chocolate hazelnut, fruity cereal and cookie butter
- Get invited to pop-up events around town
- Decorate the lot, earn stickers for mochi's journal, and hire a crew
- Great days can earn a coupon to redeem at the real domochi trailer!

No ads. No accounts. Just donuts.

Made with love for domochi, Lafayette, LA. Find the real trailer, menu and hours at domochitreats.com and on Instagram @domochi.treats.
